Testimonies and narratives from indentured labourers offer invaluable insights into the lived experiences under the indenture system.

These personal accounts, often articulated through "testimonios," provide a stark contrast to the official records and reports that were usually influenced by colonial interests.

One of the most notable testimonies is from Totaram Sanadhya, an Indian indentured labourer who documented his experiences in Fiji.

His narratives, such as the stories of Laliya and Guljari, highlight the deceptive practices used by recruiters known as arkatis who lured laborers with false promises.

Sanadhya's testimonio depicts the agonizing journey of Laliya, who traveled to Fiji with her daughter in search of her husband Ismail, showcasing the human toll of indenture.
