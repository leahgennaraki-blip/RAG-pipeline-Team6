This connection is not implied as a direct link to the Indian nation-state per se but to the cultural heritage that has been preserved over generations.

Simultaneously, their identity as Surinamese citizens is shaped by the multicultural and multiethnic environment of Suriname.

In summary, the politics of identity for Hindustani communities in Suriname is a negotiation between preserving ethnic heritage and integrating into the Surinamese multicultural national fabric.

While the affinity with Indian culture remains significant, the dynamics of Surinamese society demand a reconstructed identity that respects both past heritage and present home.
