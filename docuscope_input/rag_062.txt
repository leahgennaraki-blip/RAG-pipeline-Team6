**Purpose:** The goal of such narratives often aims at documenting and rationalizing the functioning of the colonial labor system.

It might highlight the economic contributions of indentured laborers while minimizing or ignoring abuses and hardships they faced.

**Collective Remembering:**
1.

**Sources:** Collective remembering generally draws from oral traditions, folk songs, testimonios, personal stories, and cultural expressions like music and festivals that have been passed down through generations.

These narratives are embedded in cultural practices and everyday life, as well as personal memories and community history.

2.

**Perspective:** This form of memory provides a bottom-up view, focusing on the lived experiences of the indentured laborers and their descendants.

It highlights the emotional, psychological, and social dimensions of their experiences, including the sense of alienation, identity, resilience, and community dynamics.

It may also address the preservation of cultural identity and adaptation in the diaspora.

3.

**Purpose:** Collective memories aim to retain and honor the heritage of the indentured communities.

They serve to reinforce cultural identity, commemorate the struggles and resilience of ancestors, and provide a counter-narrative to official histories.

They may also play a role in shaping contemporary cultural identity and social dynamics within diaspora communities.
