There are also calls for apology and compensation from the Dutch government and the colonial authorities, as many feel that the trauma of indenture has not been sufficiently addressed or apologized for.

8.

Indo-Surinamese Contribution to National Identity
The Indo-Surinamese community has also contributed to the broader Surinamese identity.

Many have integrated into Surinamese society, contributing to the cultural mosaic of the country.

As Suriname has grown more diverse, the contributions of the Indian community in the fields of agriculture, politics, education, and culture are celebrated, as well as their role in the nation-building process.

Conclusion: A Legacy of Duality
The legacy of indenture in Suriname is one of resilience and survival, marked by a complex interplay of suffering and cultural preservation.

For the Indian community in Suriname, the indenture system is a painful but essential part of their history, shaping their identity, culture, and social structure.

While there is a great deal of pride in the successes and advances of their ancestors, there is also an awareness of the brutality and injustice of the system.
