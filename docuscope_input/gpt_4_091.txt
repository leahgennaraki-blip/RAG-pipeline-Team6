b.

Calls for Apology and Recognition
On a collective level, descendants demand a formal apology from the Dutch government for its role in perpetuating the indenture system and for the physical and emotional abuse that indentured laborers endured.

A common sentiment is that the government’s silence on the issue is an ongoing insult to the dignity of the community.

Ravi, a community leader in Suriname, has spoken publicly about the need for the Dutch state to recognize the trauma of indenture, stating, "We don’t want just an apology.

We want our ancestors to be honored with the dignity they were denied in life." Ravi’s words reflect the common view within the community that recognition is the first step towards repairing the harm done to the generations of laborers who were systemically exploited.

2.

Economic Reparations
Another key demand of the Indo-Surinamese community is for economic reparations to compensate for the labor exploitation and the ongoing economic disadvantages faced by their descendants.
