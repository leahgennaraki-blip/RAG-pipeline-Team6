Interracial relationships on the plantations in Suriname were a well-documented phenomenon and covered a broad array of dynamics and societal receptions.

These relationships occurred despite a complex background of racial hierarchies and legal restrictions, and the ways they were received varied significantly depending on the context and the involved communities.

### Prevalence and Legal Context

In Suriname, as in many other parts of the Americas, interracial relationships were common, particularly between white men and Black or colored women.
