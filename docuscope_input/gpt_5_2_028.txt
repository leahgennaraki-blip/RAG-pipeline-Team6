Critically discussing colonial language

In Summary
Term What it Means
Coolie A vague, racialized colonial label for non-European labor
Indentured labourer A worker legally bound by a fixed-term labor contract

Relationship:

All indentured labourers were called “coolies” by colonizers,
but “coolie” does not accurately describe their legal or human reality.

In Suriname, Chinese and Indian labourers were both recruited to solve the post-emancipation labor “problem,” but they were treated differently in law, practice, and long-term outcomes.

These differences reflected colonial racial hierarchies, economic calculations, and gendered assumptions, not humanitarian concern.
