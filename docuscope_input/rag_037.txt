**Cultural and Racial Prejudice**:
- The judicial system was influenced by prevalent racist attitudes, which dehumanized indentured laborers and regarded their complaints with skepticism or outright dismissal.

This systemic bias hampered any chance of justice for the laborers.

In summary, the plantation system effectively utilized a combination of physical, economic, and legal measures to maintain control over indentured laborers.

The judicial system was designed in a manner that reinforced this control, providing planters with tools to exploit laborers with little fear of retribution, while laborers had minimal recourse to justice and protection under the law.
