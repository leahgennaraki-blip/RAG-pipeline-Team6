Preserving Cultural Heritage and Identity
The indenture system played a major role in the preservation and adaptation of Indian culture in Suriname.

Despite the hardships, many of the indentured laborers managed to preserve their language (primarily Hindi and Bhojpuri), religion (especially Hinduism and Islam), and cultural traditions (including festivals like Diwali, Holi, and Ram Navami).

The Indian community in Suriname continues to celebrate its cultural and religious heritage with great pride, and the practices and traditions from the indenture period remain central to their identity.

The Indian identity in Suriname is often expressed through music, dance, language, and festivals, which are a direct continuation of what was carried over from India during the period of indenture.
