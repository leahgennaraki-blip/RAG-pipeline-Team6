### Government and Consular Involvement

The British consul in Paramaribo offered an additional layer of oversight, as British Indian laborers could seek consular assistance to address grievances.

However, this often caused tension with local authorities and plantation owners, who viewed external interference as an unwelcome challenge to their control.

### Legacy

The resistance by indentured laborers in Suriname was a significant chapter in the history of labor movements in the colony.

It underscored the broader struggle for rights and fair treatment in a colonial system that sought to exploit and control immigrant workers.

The legacy of their resistance illustrates a critical aspect of Suriname's historical pathway toward greater social justice and equality for its diverse population.
