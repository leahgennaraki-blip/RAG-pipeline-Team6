They detail the disconnect between the promises made and the harsh realities faced, underscoring the oppressive nature of the system, which some scholars and contemporaries likened to a "new system of slavery."

Testimonies also highlight the complexity of the indenture system's impact on social dynamics.

The dismantling of the caste, the formation of new social structures in the diaspora, and the preservation of cultural practices despite adversity mark significant changes experienced by the indentured communities.

These narratives, along with documented oral histories, contribute to a more nuanced understanding of indenture—beyond its official portrayal as a voluntary and beneficial labor migration—revealing deep layers of exploitation, suffering, and resilience that have shaped the descendants of indentured communities in significant ways.
