The treatment of Chinese and Indian indentured laborers in Suriname during the colonial period exhibited significant differences influenced by factors such as recruitment, labor expectations, and racial attitudes.

### Recruitment and Arrival
1.

**Chinese Laborers**:
- The recruitment of Chinese laborers began in 1853, when Suriname's colonial government imported 18 Chinese laborers from Java.

Attempts to bring more Chinese laborers directly from China occurred in 1858 but were not expanded upon due to cost concerns and uncooperative local conditions.
