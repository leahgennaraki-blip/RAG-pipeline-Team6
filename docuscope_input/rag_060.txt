- Despite improvements, many descendants of indentured laborers continue to face challenges related to identity, socio-economic status, and the remnants of colonial racial policies.

In summary, the abolition of the indentured labor system marked the beginning of a complex legacy for the Indian diaspora in the former colonies.

While it ended a harsh and exploitative labor system, it ushered in a new era of cultural persistence blended with struggles for integration and recognition in their adopted homelands.
