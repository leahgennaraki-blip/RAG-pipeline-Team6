For authentic, firsthand accounts and detailed studies on this subject, I recommend the following sources:

Brij V.

Lal's "Chalo Jahaji: On a Journey Through Indenture in Fiji": This work delves into the lives of indentured laborers in Fiji, including personal narratives.

An excerpt titled "Kunti's Cry" offers insight into a woman's experience during this period.

Gaiutra Bahadur's "Coolie Woman: The Odyssey of Indenture": This book explores the journey of the author's great-grandmother and, more broadly, the experiences of indentured women in the Caribbean.

It combines archival research with oral histories to shed light on these women's lives.

"Unsilencing Hi(stories) of Indo-Caribbean Women": This research focuses on bringing to light the oral and written experiences of Indo-Caribbean women, addressing the historical silencing of their narratives.
