Cultural and Emotional Connection
Although Indo-Surinamese people are Surinamese nationals, many maintain a strong cultural affinity with India.

For example:

Religious practices, such as Hinduism, remain a significant part of the identity of many Indo-Surinamese, and India is often seen as the cultural homeland.

Language is another bridge between Indo-Surinamese people and India.

While many Indo-Surinamese people speak Dutch and Sranan Tongo (the national language of Suriname), there is also a strong retention of Bhojpuri and Hindi.

These languages are seen as a vital connection to their Indian ancestry.

Economic and Political Ties with India
Though the historical ties to India remain important, the level of political or economic affinity with India has been more complex.

While India has maintained a general interest in the Indian diaspora (including Indo-Surinamese people), this connection is not as pronounced as in other countries with larger Indian populations (such as Fiji or Trinidad).

However, there have been some signs of growing diplomatic ties between India and Suriname, particularly as Suriname's Indian-origin population has increasingly been seen as a link between the two nations.
