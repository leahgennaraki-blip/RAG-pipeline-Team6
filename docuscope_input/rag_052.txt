**Transformation in Family Structures**: Initially, Hindustani families tried to maintain some traditional family structures like joint families and arranged marriages, but these structures gradually lost their appeal due to urbanization, exposure to other cultural influences, and evolving economic conditions, causing a shift towards more nuclear family setups and individual choice in marriage.

In conclusion, while the caste system in its traditional form did not reinvent itself in Suriname, its influence largely diminished due to the new social dynamics and cultural exchanges in the colony.

Any residual elements of caste appeared in transformed ways within a society that prioritized new forms of community organization and identity, primarily driven by the integration into a multicultural society.
